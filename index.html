Context of Advertising

Advertising is a paid, impersonal and sponsor-identified form of communication used to promote goods or services and persuade potential customers to buy them.

Introduction

Meaning of advertising

Advertising is an impersonal form of communication, paid for by marketers or sponsors, to promote goods or services. Common media include newspapers, magazines, television and radio.

Features

Essential features

  • Paid form: The sponsor bears the cost of communication.
  • Impersonality: There is no direct face-to-face contact.
  • Identified sponsor: An identifiable person or company makes and pays for the advertisement.
Merits

Main advantages

  • Mass reach across a wide geographical area.
  • Enhances customer satisfaction and confidence.
  • Expressive through visuals, sound and graphics.
  • Economical for reaching a large audience.
Objections

Major objections

  • It may add to product cost.
  • It may undermine social values.
  • It may confuse buyers.
  • It may encourage the sale of inferior products.
Conclusion: Advertising informs, persuades and influences consumers. It is an essential marketing tool, but its effectiveness depends on using the right combination of promotional tools.
Page 03

Features and Functions

Advertising has three main features and performs several important functions in marketing communication.

Feature 1

Paid form

The sponsor must bear the cost of communicating with prospective customers.

Feature 2

Impersonality

There is no direct face-to-face contact between the advertiser and the prospect. Advertising creates a monologue rather than a dialogue.

Feature 3

Identified sponsor

An identifiable individual or company makes the advertisement and bears its cost.

Functions of advertising

Informing customers

Advertising informs potential customers about the availability, features and merits of a product or service.

Creating awareness and interest

Advertising creates awareness and builds interest among prospective buyers.

Persuading customers

As part of promotion, advertising helps persuade potential customers to buy a product or service.

Supporting mass marketing

Mass media enables a standardised message to reach a large number of consumers in a market segment within a short time.

Conclusion

Advertising is an important promotional tool. It informs potential customers, creates interest and persuades them to purchase goods or services.

Strengths

Why advertising matters

  • Mass reach across large geographical areas.
  • Enhances customer satisfaction and confidence.
  • Expressive through visuals, sound and graphics.
  • Economical when reaching a large audience.
Limitations

Where advertising falls short

  • Less forceful than personal selling.
  • Lacks direct and immediate feedback.
  • Inflexible message.
  • Lower effectiveness in certain selling situations.
Advertising informs, persuades and influences—but effective marketing requires the right communication mix.
